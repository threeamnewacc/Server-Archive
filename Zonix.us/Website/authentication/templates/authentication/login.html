You'll need to connect to our server and register. Follow the instructions below to get started!
Step #1

Connect to one of our servers and use the command /register <email>. Make sure you're using an email address that you have access to.

If you used an incorrect email when signing up, wait 2 minutes and use the register command again. If you are currently banned, you can still join our hub servers.
Step #2

Login to your email and look for a confirmation email from site@zonix.us. Click on it and follow the instructions inside to confirm your account on our website.

Ensure that the password that you are creating is strong and is not used elsewhere for maximum security.
Step #3

That's it! Come back to this page and input your login information on the left side. Once you're logged in, you can create support tickets, apply for staff, and more.
